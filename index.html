HospitalBench 2027

Diagnosing Long-Horizon Failure in Embodied Agents · Seattle, 20 or 21 June 2027

Where, and why, does an embodied agent's performance degrade along a chain of dependent tasks? HospitalBench 2027 is a proposed workshop and challenge on ten simulated hospital tasks of chained sub-tasks, each link checked by moving the previous result. Beside end-task success, every entry gets a failure-mode profile read from its logged trajectories: compounding errors per link, recovery, state drift and tool-use breakdowns, with per-position curves and task-length scaling. Two tracks, foundation models and vision-language-action policies, are scored per sub-task and per link. Harm events are simulator proxies, not injuries.
